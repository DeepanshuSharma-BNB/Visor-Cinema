Visor Cinema

Private link sender · Phone controller

01 · Pair your glasses

Open Visor Cinema on your glasses and enter the 12-character code shown on the display. Your phone does not need to be the video source.

Not paired. No link has been sent.

02 · Send a video link

Copy the video URL from any browser and paste it here. Visor Cinema will attempt direct media playback or a permitted webpage embed on the glasses.

Pair your glasses first.
The phone sends an encrypted link, not video frames. The glasses fetch and attempt to play the content themselves. A website can still block playback, embedding, or autoplay.

03 · Remote controls

These commands work with direct video files. Embedded players may not respond to remote controls.

No accounts. No locally saved viewing history. Link messages are encrypted in the browser before going through a third-party public relay (ntfy.sh), which can see connection metadata but not decrypted link content. The recipient needs the current pairing code. Closing the glasses app generates a new code on its next launch. This experimental service may be rate-limited or unavailable.
